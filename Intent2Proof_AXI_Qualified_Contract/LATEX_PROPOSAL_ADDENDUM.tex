\subsection{Repairing and testing a cycle-accurate AXI-Lite control contract}
Following the observed address-acceptance mismatch between a combinational-ready
candidate contract and the registered-ready upstream AXI-Lite interconnect,
we constructed a read-only control abstraction that preserves the original
arbitration mechanism, registered address/response handshake timing,
address decoding, response-status routing, and backpressure behavior.
The miter compares two master ports and two destination ports under matched
exogenous inputs. Its observations comprise address-ready, read-response-valid,
downstream address-valid, downstream response-ready, response code when valid,
and downstream address when valid. Read data is explicitly excluded.
Seven source-anchored, one-step Z3 snapshots yielded their expected SAT/UNSAT
outcomes, and 14 harness regression tests passed. The complete evaluation
includes seven unbounded miter proofs, three non-vacuity covers, and two
negative controls against unmodified, pinned upstream RTL. At the time of
this addendum, these 12 SymbiYosys tasks were prepared but not executed in
this environment. Thus no full-RTL refinement proof, general trace-inclusion
claim, or solver-speedup measurement is asserted. Approval remains blocked
until the proof conditions and scope-specific assumption checks are discharged.
