\subsection{Protocol-Aware AXI-Lite Refinement: Benchmark Extension}
The initial read-control refinement miter had shown cycle-equivalence under
its declared assumptions, but its contention covers admitted unsolicited
responses. We therefore developed a two-slave, transaction-aware environment
in which each response can follow only a prior downstream
\texttt{ARVALID}\,$\land$\,\texttt{ARREADY} acceptance. An independent
scoreboard checks outstanding reads and response stability under backpressure.
The revised cover requires two downstream read-address acceptances, two
matched downstream response completions, and completion for both masters.
Five read-control equivalence proof tasks, four legal transaction covers,
and three fault-injection checks are specified for the pinned open-source
\texttt{axil\_interconnect} and the manually authored v2 contract.

\textbf{Evidence boundary.} Local Python reference-model exploration checked
3 and 9 reachable protocol-control states for one and two slaves, respectively,
and detected an unsolicited-response fault. These are not RTL proofs. Full RTL
Yosys/SymbiYosys execution and independent witness audit remain necessary;
no improved solver runtime, automatic abstraction, write-path correctness, or
general trace inclusion is claimed on the basis of the local experiment.
