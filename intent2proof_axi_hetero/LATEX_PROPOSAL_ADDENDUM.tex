\subsection{Heterogeneous AXI-Lite integration: preliminary architectural evaluation}
We next examined a two-master, two-slave read-only AXI-Lite system with
arbiter selection, destination routing, response backpressure and a shutdown
controller. The full-RTL study targets the open-source \texttt{axil\_interconnect}
from the \texttt{verilog-axi} project, together with its arbitration dependencies
and locally authored slave controllers. The present executed results are from
an independently constructed protocol-level transition system, not from an
elaborated RTL model.

For each master $i\in\{0,1\}$, the monitor credit is incremented on
$\mathrm{ARVALID}_i\land\mathrm{ARREADY}_i$ and decremented on
$\mathrm{RVALID}_i\land\mathrm{RREADY}_i$. The global intent requires
that reconfiguration never start with an outstanding credit or a pending
read address. The model exercises both response backpressure and limited
slave latency nondeterminism. At waiting bounds $1,2,3,4$, exhaustive
exploration reached $90,114,138,162$ distinct states respectively, and found
no violation in the correct model. A mutation omitting master~1 from the
shutdown guard had a two-transition counterexample for all four settings.
A second mutation that misroutes a response reached a counterexample after
three transitions. Five native-Z3 one-step contract checks returned the
expected four consistency/negative-control outcomes: three UNSAT and two SAT.
