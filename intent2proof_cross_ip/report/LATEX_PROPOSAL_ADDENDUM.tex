\subsection{Cross-IP streaming and shutdown: preliminary contract-composition evaluation}
We constructed a same-clock streaming subsystem containing three instances of
ZipCPU's pinned open-source \texttt{sfifo} IP, with a separately authored
round-robin merge controller and reconfiguration shutdown gate. The approved
system property is \(\mathbf G(\mathit{reconfig\_start}\!\Rightarrow\!P=0)\),
where \(P\) is independently reconstructed from accepted ingress transfers
minus completed sink transfers. FIFO interface contracts specify occupancy,
full/empty flags, and legal handshake updates. For capacities 4, 8, and 16,
exhaustive exploration of manually source-derived control equations visited
354, 2,290, and 17,106 reachable states respectively. All healthy models
satisfied the safety property; removing the sink-emptiness test from the
shutdown guard generated a feasible two-transition counterexample in each
configuration. Twelve independent native-Z3 checks confirmed the expected
SAT/UNSAT statuses of the abstract one-step conservation, capacity and
shutdown obligations, including negative controls.

\emph{Scope and limitations:} The above are executed control-model and
contract-composition checks, not direct proofs of the newly integrated RTL.
The count-only SMT encoding was smaller than a conventional COI encoding,
but measured median six-step solver time did not improve (0.234 s versus
0.204 s). A new 18-task SBY benchmark has been generated for the pinned
full RTL, standard COI, proposed contract abstraction, independent unit
contract-discharge, mutant, and non-vacuity cover checks. It remains
unexecuted in the local runtime. No system-level RTL proof or proof-time
speedup is claimed until that benchmark completes.
